# Figure data attribution

`04_representative_t0_t3_c1b_h_vs_r.png` and its descriptive alias
`registration_representative_hr.png` are modified, de-identified technical-review
panels derived from public breast MRI data distributed by The Cancer Imaging
Archive (TCIA):

- Li, W., et al. (2022). *I-SPY 2 Breast Dynamic Contrast Enhanced MRI Trial
  (ISPY2)* (Version 1) [Data set]. TCIA.
  <https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.D8Z0-9T85>
- Newitt, D. C., et al. (2021). *ACRIN 6698/I-SPY2 Breast DWI* [Data set].
  TCIA. <https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.KK02-6D95>

The source collections are licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes made here
include DICOM reconstruction, canonical reorientation, fixed-grid resampling,
intensity normalization, anonymous size-stratum selection, rigid-registration
comparison, cropping, compositing, difference-map coloring, and technical
annotations. No patient identifiers are included. These modifications are the
authors' own and do not imply endorsement by TCIA or the source-data authors.

The remaining numbered figures are aggregate QC plots and contain no source
image pixels.
